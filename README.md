# Arshad Electronics · demo 2

demo 1 plus the changes from the client meeting of 17 October 2026, built on 26 October 2026. It is for client review and is not the live site.

## What's new since demo 1

- **Contact details:** The landlines are gone. The footer shows both; the Simco-Ion page shows the first. The office address includes the pincode and has an "Open in Google Maps" link.
- **Affiliations:** Plasmaright replaces Anvertech.
- **Homepage FAQ:** two tabs, Corona treaters and Cap sealers.
- **Announcements:** a bell in the header with a +1 for anything new. A new announcement pops up once per visitor.
- **Google reviews:** the rating and the selected reviews, shown on the homepage.
- **Product videos:** a click-to-play YouTube video in each product's specs.
- **Admin** (`admin/`): one login to edit announcements, reviews, contact details and videos.

Announcements, reviews and videos only show once something is entered in the admin.
